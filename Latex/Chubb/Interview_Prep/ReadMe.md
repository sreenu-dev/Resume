## 💡 Interview Tips for Senior Role

### What Chubb Expects
1. **Architecture & Design**: Can design scalable systems
2. **Code Quality**: Writes clean, maintainable code
3. **Leadership**: Mentors junior developers
4. **Problem-Solving**: Analyzes complex issues
5. **Production Support**: Handles critical issues efficiently
6. **Communication**: Explains concepts clearly

### During the Interview
- ✅ Explain your thinking process
- ✅ Consider trade-offs and alternatives
- ✅ Write clean, readable code
- ✅ Test your solutions thoroughly
- ✅ Discuss optimization opportunities
- ✅ Use real-world examples
- ✅ Ask clarifying questions

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- ❌ Not reading the problem completely
- ❌ Jumping to code without planning
- ❌ Forgetting edge cases
- ❌ Not testing the solution
- ❌ Poor communication
- ❌ Inefficient algorithms
- ❌ Not considering scalability
