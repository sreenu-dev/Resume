## The 3 Things That Win at HSBC (Ranked)

### 1. Global Perspective & Teamwork (Most Important)
HSBC is a truly global bank. They care deeply about your ability to work across cultures, time zones, and geographies.

Every story should include:
- How did you work with people from different backgrounds?
- How did you handle time zone differences?
- How did you respect diverse perspectives?

**In behavioral:** "I worked with a global team across [regions]..."
**In system design:** "This system works across regions with different regulatory requirements..."
**In coding:** Show you understand distributed systems at global scale.

### 2. HSBC Values Alignment (Second Most Important)
HSBC has 5 core values: Integrity, Customer Focus, Teamwork, Excellence, Sustainability.

Every story should map to at least one value:
- **Integrity:** ethical decisions, compliance
- **Customer Focus:** user impact
- **Teamwork:** collaboration
- **Excellence:** raising the bar
- **Sustainability:** long-term thinking

### 3. Technical Depth in .NET/Azure (Third Most Important)
Your .NET/Azure stack is perfect for HSBC. Show you understand cloud-native architecture, Kubernetes, and global system design.

## During Each Round — HSBC-Specific Cheat Sheet

### Coding Rounds
```
Opening every coding problem:
"Before I start — I want to understand the scale and context. 
 Is this a global system? Are there regulatory considerations? 
 [This signals you think about real-world constraints]"

After coding, proactively say:
"Let me also consider global implications:
 - Data residency: if this were global, I'd ensure data stays in the right region
 - Consistency: I'd use eventual consistency for global scale
 - Compliance: I'd add audit logging for regulatory requirements"
```

### System Design Round
```
Always open with global framing:
"Before I start, I want to establish the global scope — specifically 
 around data residency and regulatory compliance, because HSBC operates 
 across 70+ countries with different requirements.
 
 The non-negotiables I'll design for:
 1. Data residency — EU data stays in EU, India data in India
 2. Multi-region failover — survive entire region failure
 3. Compliance — GDPR, RBI, PCI-DSS across all regions
 
 Does that align with what you're looking for?"
```

### Behavioral Round
```
STAR + Value format:
  Situation: 15 seconds
  Task:      15 seconds
  Action:    90 seconds (include: how you collaborated, how you respected diversity)
  Result:    20 seconds (always a metric)
  Value:     15 seconds ("This demonstrates HSBC's value of [Teamwork/Integrity/etc]")

HSBC-specific language to weave in:
  "I worked with a global team across [regions]..."
  "I respected diverse perspectives and..."
  "This demonstrates HSBC's value of [value name]..."
  "I thought about long-term sustainability..."
  "I prioritized compliance and integrity..."
```

## Offer Negotiation at HSBC

### The HSBC Compensation Structure
- **Base salary** — fixed
- **Annual bonus** — discretionary, 15–25% of base
- **Stock options** — for senior roles
- **Benefits:** medical, pension, employee banking benefits
- **Relocation:** if moving to India

### Negotiation Script
```
Step 1 — Ask for time:
"Thank you for the offer. I'm very excited about joining HSBC. 
 Could I have 3 working days to review the details carefully?"

Step 2 — Counter:
"I've done my research on Senior Software Engineer compensation 
 for this location and skill profile. Based on my experience in 
 [global systems, compliance architecture, cloud-native design], 
 I was hoping we could discuss [₹X LPA] for the base, with a 
 corresponding variable target. Is there flexibility there?"

Step 3 — If they push back:
"I understand. If the base is fixed, is there flexibility on 
 the joining bonus or stock options to bridge the gap? I'm very 
 motivated to join HSBC and want to find a structure that works 
 for both sides."
```
