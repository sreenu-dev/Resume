# HSBC Culture & Values — The Complete Guide

## Who HSBC Actually Is

HSBC is **the world's largest bank by assets** (over $3 trillion) and operates in **70+ countries**. It is not a US-centric tech company like Amazon or Microsoft. It is a **truly global financial institution** that is aggressively modernizing its technology to compete with fintech and cloud-native competitors.

> **HSBC's Strategic Vision:** "From a global bank to a bank for a global world" — emphasizing digital-first, customer-centric, and technology-enabled banking.

---

## HSBC's Core Values (The Foundation)

HSBC's values are embedded in everything they do. Interviewers assess you against these:

### Value 1: Integrity
> *Do the right thing, always. Transparency and ethical behavior.*

**For engineers:** Security and compliance are non-negotiable. You don't cut corners on data protection or regulatory requirements, even under deadline pressure.

**Interview signal:** Talk about times you advocated for security/compliance even when inconvenient.

---

### Value 2: Customer Focus
> *Put customers first. Understand their needs and serve them excellently.*

**For engineers:** Every system you build ultimately serves customers — whether they're retail customers, corporate clients, or internal teams.

**Interview signal:** Discuss how your technical decisions traced back to customer impact.

---

### Value 3: Teamwork
> *Collaborate across boundaries. Respect diverse perspectives. Build together.*

**For engineers:** HSBC is global — you work with teams across time zones, cultures, and geographies. Silos are the enemy.

**Interview signal:** Stories about cross-team collaboration, especially across geographies or cultures.

---

### Value 4: Excellence
> *Raise the bar. Pursue quality and continuous improvement.*

**For engineers:** Technical excellence is expected. Code quality, testing, and operational maturity matter.

**Interview signal:** Examples of raising standards, mentoring, or improving processes.

---

### Value 5: Sustainability
> *Think long-term. Consider environmental and social impact.*

**For engineers:** HSBC cares about responsible banking, ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance), and sustainable technology practices.

**Interview signal:** Mention energy-efficient systems, responsible AI, or sustainable practices if relevant.

---

## HSBC's Global Perspective — What Makes It Different

Unlike Amazon (US-centric), Microsoft (US-centric), JPMC (US-centric), or Wells Fargo (US-centric), **HSBC is truly global**. This changes how they think about engineering:

### Multi-Region Thinking
- Systems must work across time zones
- Data residency requirements vary by country (GDPR in EU, RBI in India, etc.)
- Regulatory requirements differ by jurisdiction
- Currency and settlement rules are complex

### Cultural Diversity
- HSBC values engineers from diverse backgrounds
- Cross-cultural communication is a strength, not a weakness
- Global teams are the norm, not the exception

### Regulatory Complexity
- HSBC operates under 70+ different regulatory regimes
- Compliance is not a constraint — it's a competitive advantage
- Engineers must understand regulatory context of their work

## HSBC's Technology Transformation — What They're Building

HSBC is in the middle of a massive digital transformation. Understanding this context helps in interviews:

### 1. Cloud Migration (Azure-First)
- Moving from on-premises to Azure
- Hybrid cloud approach (Azure + AWS + on-premises)
- Containerization with Kubernetes

### 2. Open Banking / API-First
- Building APIs for fintech partnerships
- Open Banking standards (PSD2 in EU, RBI in India)
- Ecosystem play — not just internal systems

### 3. AI/ML for Banking
- Fraud detection and prevention
- Compliance automation (AML/KYC)
- Customer service chatbots
- Predictive analytics

### 4. Legacy Modernization
- Strangler pattern — gradually replacing legacy systems
- Microservices replacing monoliths
- Cloud-native architecture

### 5. Cybersecurity & Zero-Trust
- Advanced threat detection
- Zero-trust security model
- API security and rate limiting
