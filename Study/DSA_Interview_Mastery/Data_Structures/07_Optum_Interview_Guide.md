# Optum Interview Guide

## Company Overview
- **Industry**: Healthcare and Technology
- **Focus**: Healthcare IT, data analytics, cloud solutions, AI/ML in healthcare
- **Interview Style**: Algorithm-heavy, system design, healthcare domain knowledge
- **Difficulty**: Medium to Hard
- **Time Limit**: 45-60 minutes per problem

## Interview Process
1. **Phone Screen** (1 round): 1 medium problem + behavioral questions
2. **On-Site** (4-5 rounds):
   - 2-3 Coding rounds
   - 1-2 System Design rounds
   - 1 Behavioral round
