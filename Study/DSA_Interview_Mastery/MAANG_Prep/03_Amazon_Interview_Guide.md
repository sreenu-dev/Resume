# Amazon Interview Guide

## Company Overview
- **Focus**: Scalability, customer obsession, operational excellence
- **Interview Style**: Practical, system design, behavioral
- **Difficulty**: Medium to Hard
- **Time Limit**: 45 minutes per problem

## Interview Process
1. **Phone Screen** (1 round): 1 medium problem
2. **On-Site** (4-5 rounds):
   - 2 Coding rounds
   - 1-2 System Design rounds
   - 1 Behavioral round
