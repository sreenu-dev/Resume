# JPMorgan Chase (JPMC) Interview Guide

## Company Overview
- **Industry**: Banking and Financial Services
- **Focus**: Trading systems, risk management, fintech, scalability
- **Interview Style**: Algorithm-heavy, system design, financial domain
- **Difficulty**: Hard
- **Time Limit**: 45-60 minutes per problem

## Interview Process
1. **Phone Screen** (1 round): 1 medium-hard problem + behavioral
2. **On-Site** (4-5 rounds):
   - 2-3 Coding rounds
   - 1-2 System Design rounds
   - 1 Behavioral round
