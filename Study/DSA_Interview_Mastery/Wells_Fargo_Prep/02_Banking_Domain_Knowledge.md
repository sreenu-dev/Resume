# Banking Domain Knowledge for Wells Fargo

## Overview
Understanding banking concepts is crucial for Wells Fargo interviews. This guide covers essential financial and banking knowledge.

## Core Banking Concepts

### 1. Types of Accounts
- **Checking Accounts**: Daily transactions, debit cards
- **Savings Accounts**: Interest-bearing, withdrawal limits
- **Money Market Accounts**: Higher interest, minimum balance
- **Certificates of Deposit (CDs)**: Fixed term, fixed rate
- **Investment Accounts**: Stocks, bonds, mutual funds
- **Retirement Accounts**: 401(k), IRA, Roth IRA

### 2. Interest Calculations

#### Simple Interest
```
Formula: I = P × R × T
Where:
  I = Interest
  P = Principal
  R = Rate (annual)
  T = Time (years)
```

#### Compound Interest
```
Formula: A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt)
Where:
  A = Final amount
  P = Principal
  r = Annual rate
  n = Compounding periods per year
  t = Time in years
```

#### APR vs APY
- **APR (Annual Percentage Rate)**: Nominal rate
- **APY (Annual Percentage Yield)**: Effective rate including compounding

### 3. Loan Types
- **Mortgages**: Long-term, secured by property
- **Auto Loans**: Vehicle financing
- **Personal Loans**: Unsecured, fixed rate
- **Student Loans**: Education financing
- **Credit Cards**: Revolving credit

### 4. Loan Calculations

#### Amortization
```
Monthly Payment = P × [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n - 1]
Where:
  P = Principal
  r = Monthly interest rate
  n = Number of payments
```

#### Loan-to-Value (LTV)
```
LTV = Loan Amount / Property Value
Used for: Risk assessment, mortgage approval
```

## Financial Instruments

### 1. Bonds
- **Definition**: Fixed income security
- **Types**: Government, corporate, municipal
- **Yield**: Return on investment
- **Duration**: Interest rate sensitivity

### 2. Stocks
- **Definition**: Ownership in company
- **Types**: Common, preferred
- **Dividend**: Periodic payments to shareholders
- **Price-to-Earnings (P/E)**: Valuation metric

### 3. Mutual Funds
- **Definition**: Pooled investment vehicle
- **Types**: Index, actively managed
- **Expense Ratio**: Annual cost
- **NAV**: Net Asset Value

### 4. Derivatives
- **Options**: Right to buy/sell at price
- **Futures**: Obligation to buy/sell at price
- **Swaps**: Exchange of cash flows
- **Forwards**: Customized futures

## Risk Management

### 1. Types of Risk
- **Credit Risk**: Borrower default
- **Market Risk**: Price fluctuations
- **Liquidity Risk**: Inability to sell quickly
- **Operational Risk**: Internal failures
- **Regulatory Risk**: Compliance issues

### 2. Risk Metrics
- **Value at Risk (VaR)**: Maximum potential loss
- **Standard Deviation**: Volatility measure
- **Beta**: Systematic risk measure
- **Sharpe Ratio**: Risk-adjusted return

### 3. Risk Mitigation
- **Diversification**: Spread investments
- **Hedging**: Offset potential losses
- **Insurance**: Transfer risk
- **Reserves**: Capital buffers
