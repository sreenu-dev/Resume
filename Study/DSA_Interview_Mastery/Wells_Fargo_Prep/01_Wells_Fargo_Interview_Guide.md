# Wells Fargo Interview Guide

## Company Overview
- **Industry**: Banking and Financial Services
- **Focus**: Risk management, compliance, security, scalability
- **Interview Style**: Practical, system design, behavioral
- **Difficulty**: Medium
- **Time Limit**: 45-60 minutes per problem

## Interview Process
1. **Phone Screen** (1 round): 1 medium problem + behavioral questions
2. **On-Site** (3-4 rounds):
   - 1-2 Coding rounds
   - 1 System Design round
   - 1 Behavioral round

## Core Values
1. **Integrity**: Ethical decision-making
2. **Diversity & Inclusion**: Inclusive environment
3. **Customer Focus**: Customer-centric solutions
4. **Accountability**: Responsibility for actions
5. **Teamwork**: Collaborative approach
